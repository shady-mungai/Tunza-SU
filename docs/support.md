# Tunza SU support

Tunza SU's production support URL, optional support email address, and privacy
URL are supplied through release environment settings. The app does not embed
an invented phone number or mailbox.

## Before publishing this page

The deploying organization must add:

- its support URL and monitored email address;
- expected support hours and response targets;
- the institution's emergency and campus-security contacts;
- its identity-verification process for account and privacy requests.

## What to include in a support request

Provide the app version, device model, operating-system version, approximate
time of the issue, report identifier if relevant, and steps that reproduce the
problem. Do not send a password, access token, identity document, or other
unnecessary sensitive information.

## Account and privacy requests

Use the published support channel for account access, correction, deletion, or
privacy questions. The operator should verify identity before changing an
account or disclosing data. Report records may be subject to documented
operational, safety, audit, or legal retention requirements.

## Service incidents

Support staff should record severity, affected release and environment,
request identifiers, timestamps, user impact, mitigation, and follow-up. Do
not copy personal report content into a public issue tracker.

## Emergencies

Tunza SU is a maintenance-reporting tool, not an emergency service. Users
should contact the institution's published emergency or campus-security
channel when there is an immediate risk to people or property.
