## Listing copy

**App name:** Tunza SU

**Subtitle / short description:** Report campus maintenance issues and follow
their progress.

**Full description:**

Tunza SU gives an authorized university community a straightforward way to
report faulty infrastructure. Students can describe an issue, choose its
location and priority, optionally attach a photo, and follow its status.
Authorized maintenance staff can manage assigned work, while administrators
coordinate reports and user access.

Key capabilities:

- submit maintenance reports with an optional image;
- follow pending, in-progress, resolved, or rejected reports;
- assign work through role-controlled staff workflows;
- receive account and report updates from the service; and
- use secure password or configured Google authentication.

Tunza SU is not an emergency service. Availability and access are controlled by
the organization operating the deployed service.

**Keywords:** campus maintenance, facilities, infrastructure, fault reporting,
student services

## Permissions and review notes

- Photo-library access is used only when the user chooses an existing report
  image.
- Camera access is used only when the user chooses to take a report image.
- Audio recording is blocked because the app does not need it.
- The reviewer needs a dedicated non-production account and an HTTPS preview
  API. Put credentials in the store's private review-notes field, never here.
- Explain any institution-only login restrictions and provide a reachable
  support contact for review.
